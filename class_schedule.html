Tips Before Attending

Arrive Early

Arrive 10–15 minutes before class to settle in and prepare.

Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water before and after your yoga session.

Wear Comfortable Clothes

Choose light and comfortable clothing that allows free movement.

Listen to Your Body

Practice at your own pace and avoid pushing beyond your limits.

Switch Off Your Phone

Keep your phone on silent to maintain a peaceful environment.
